## What is it about?

You start out as a humble squire with no armour and a wooden sword. Earn gold, brave adventures, win tournaments and defeat twelve mighty bosses until you become an **Immortal Legend** and finally a **Demigod** (or **Demigoddess**). After that the Endless Tower awaits, and with rebirth you can start over again – only stronger.

### Step by step

So the start isn't overwhelming, at first you only have **Work, Adventure, Market and Sleep**. Everything else unlocks bit by bit:

| When | Newly unlocked |
|---|---|
| Level 2 | 📜 Skills |
| Level 3 | 🎒 Inventory |
| Level 4 | 👑 Boss fights |
| Level 5 | ⚔️ Rival Sir Gregor |
| Level 6 | 📜 Daily quests |
| Level 8 | 🏟️ Tournament |
| Level 9 | 🐾 Companions |
| Level 10 | 🍺 Tavern |
| Level 12 | 🧰 Workshop and 🛒 trader |
| Level 15 | 📅 Calendar (market day, tournament day) and 🌕 full-moon hunt |
| Level 18 | 🏗️ Castle building |
| 3rd boss defeated | 🗼 Endless Tower |
| 6th boss defeated | 👑 The King's War Chest |
| All bosses defeated | 🌟 Rebirth |

Every unlock is announced with **🔓 Newly unlocked**. The new button carries a red **NEW** until you use it for the first time. Whatever you have unlocked stays unlocked, even after a rebirth.

## The castle – your main screen

On the castle screen you see:

- **Name and rank** of your knight at the top
- **Your knight** in front of the castle, wearing their current gear
- **Your stats**: strength, stamina, dexterity, energy, gold, fame, title and level
- **The message line**: it tells you what happened last.
- **The action buttons**: Work, Adventure, Market, Trader, Workshop, Inventory, Tournament, Tower, Boss fight, Full-moon hunt, Castle building, Tavern, War Chest, Rebirth and Sleep
- **Calendar and daily quests** above the scene and below the message line
- **Your skills and potions** at the bottom

## Energy and days

Each day starts with **6 energy**. With barracks it can be up to 8. Almost every activity costs energy:

| Activity | Cost |
|---|---|
| ⚒️ Work | 1 energy |
| 🧰 Crafting in the workshop | 1 energy |
| ⚔️ Adventure | 2 energy |
| 👑 Boss fight | 3 energy |
| 🌕 Full-moon hunt | 3 energy |
| 🏟️ Tournament | 4 energy + 20 gold (free on tournament day) |
| 🗼 Endless Tower | 4 energy |

When your energy runs out, click **🌙 Sleep**. A lot happens overnight:

1. **Your castle delivers yields** (see [Castle building](#castle-building)). How much depends on how busy you were during the day:
   - **at least 4 energy used** → full yields
   - **2–3 energy used** → half yields
   - **less** → nothing. Idlers get nothing.
2. **In the morning** a random event happens.
3. **New daily quests** await you.
4. A **night report** sums everything up before the new day begins.

### Morning events

Every new morning brings a random event:

- 🐓 **A quiet morning**: nothing special happens.
- 👑 **A royal messenger** brings you gold.
- 🧪 **A travelling merchant** gives you a healing potion.
- 🤺 **An old veteran** trains with you and you gain experience.
- 🎉 **On the market square** your skill is celebrated and you gain fame.
- 😴 **Slept badly**: you start with 1 less energy.
- 🦹 **Pickpockets** steal 10% of your gold overnight, but at most 50 gold + 15 per level.

Good events are more common than bad ones. The higher your level, the bigger the rewards.

### Daily quests

Every morning you get **three quests**, for example “Win 2 fights”, “Craft something in the workshop”, “Land 3 critical hits”, “Clear 2 tower floors” or “Win a game of dice”. You see them on the castle screen.

- All three quests can be done with one day's energy.
- Every completed quest gives its reward immediately: gold, materials or potions.
- If you complete **all three**, a **🎁 bonus chest** opens as well, with gold, an ether potion and materials.
- Unfinished quests expire when you go to sleep.

### Calendar

At the top of the castle screen you see the weekday, the moon phase and the next special days:

| When | Event | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Every **Saturday** | 🛒 **Market day** | Everything at the trader is 30% cheaper, potions at the market 25% |
| Every **Sunday** | 🏟️ **Tournament day** | The tournament has no fee, and winning it brings a double prize including a second piece of loot |
| Every **8 days** | 🌕 **Full moon** | The **full-moon werewolf** hunts. You get one attempt. |

### 🌕 Full-moon hunt

At full moon the **Full-moon hunt** button appears on the castle screen. The werewolf is a roaming boss at your level. Its bite is poisonous, it pounces to stun you and takes cover with its howl. You get **one attempt** per full moon.

**Loot:**
- one piece of [random loot](#enchanted-gear), at least 🔵 Rare
- plus an ether potion and arcane crystals

## Castle building

Under **🏗️ Castle building** you erect buildings with gold and materials. Building costs no energy. Each building can be upgraded several times. You then see it in the castle scene, and it delivers yields every night.

| Building | Levels | Yield per night (when fully busy) |
|---|---|---|
| ⛺ Barracks | 2 | +1 energy per level, every morning |
| 🌿 Herb garden | 5 | 2 healing herbs per level, from level 3 also toadstools |
| ⛏️ Mine | 5 | 2 iron ore per level, from level 3 also mithril ore |
| 🔮 Arcane tower | 5 | 1 arcane crystal per level, from level 4 also fire blossoms |
| 💰 Treasury | 5 | 30 gold per level |

### Buildings that grow

Every building changes its look with each level, in the castle scene and as a large picture in castle building:

- **⛺ Barracks:** from a tent with a weapon rack to a half-timbered barracks house with a campfire
- **🌿 Herb garden:** from the first bed, via fence, flowers and toadstools with a scarecrow, to a glowing greenhouse
- **⛏️ Mine:** from a simple tunnel, via mine cart, lantern and mithril ore, to a headframe with glowing veins of ore
- **🔮 Arcane tower:** from a crystal on a plinth to a tall tower with rune bands, a circling ring and a beam of light into the sky
- **💰 Treasury:** from a chest, via a stone house, to a golden dome with pillars and mountains of coins

### Decorations

In castle building you can adorn your castle for gold. Each decoration grants **fame** once and can be seen in the castle scene:

| Decoration | Price | Fame |
|---|---|---|
| 🚩 Splendid banners | 600 | +60 |
| 🏮 Lantern path | 900 | +90 |
| ⛲ Marble fountain | 1500 | +150 |
| 🗿 Hero statue (of you!) | 3000 | +300 |
| 💀 Dragon skull above the gate (after Vulkanus) | 5000 | +500 |

### Your castle grows too

The castle itself becomes grander with every upgrade. What counts is the **sum of all building levels**:

| Building levels | Castle | Look |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Homestead | two small towers and a simple gate |
| 2 | Castle | windows, portcullis and red banners |
| 5 | Stronghold | higher walls and towers, outer ramparts |
| 9 | Fortress | a mighty keep in the middle, flags on every tower |
| 14 | Citadel | pale stone, pointed tower roofs, gold-trimmed gate |
| 19 | Royal Castle | blue roofs with golden finials and warmly lit windows |

In castle building you see your castle's current stage and when the next one comes.

Building costs rise with each level. High levels need mithril ore, the last one a dragon scale too. The barracks work regardless of how busy you were.

## Activities

### ⚒️ Work
You help out in town and earn gold, with no risk at all. The higher your level, the better you're paid.

### ⚔️ Adventure
A random foe of roughly your level challenges you. A victory brings fame, gold and experience, sometimes a healing potion as well. Here you can flee if need be.

### 🏟️ Tournament
Three fights in a row, each opponent a little stronger than the last. Between rounds you recover 30% of your HP. Fleeing is not possible.

If you win all three fights, you become **tournament champion** and receive:
- a 🏆 trophy
- +100 gold, +30 fame, +50 XP
- one piece of [random loot](#enchanted-gear)

See [Bosses](#bosses).

See [The Endless Tower](#the-endless-tower).

## Combat

Combat takes turns in rounds. You go first, then the enemy. You have three options:

- **⚔️ Fight**: you choose one of your skills.
- **🎒 Potions**: you drink a potion. This costs your turn.
- **🏃 Flee**: only possible on normal adventures. The chance is 50% and rises with dexterity up to at most 90%. If you fail, the enemy attacks.

### Good to know

- **Critical hits** deal 50% more damage. The chance rises with your dexterity, up to at most 60%.
- **Dodging**: with high dexterity you dodge attacks more often. An enemy always hits with at least 25% probability, though.
- **Guard**: some enemies take cover. Your next attack then deals only half damage.
- **Armour and shield** reduce every hit you take.
- **Limited skills**: strong skills can only be used a few times per fight (e.g. “2/2×”). The Sword Strike, on the other hand, can be used as often as you like.

### ⚡ Quick fight and keyboard shortcuts

Against normal foes you can use the **quick fight**. The fight then runs automatically with the Sword Strike, and you drink healing potions when your HP is low. If it gets too close, the quick fight stops and you decide yourself. There's no quick fight against bosses, the rival or mimics.

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1–7 | Use skill 1–7 (the Sword Strike is 1) |
| K | Skills menu |
| T | Potions menu |
| S | Quick fight |
| Esc | back |
| Enter | after the fight: continue |

### Victory and defeat

| | Result |
|---|---|
| 🏆 **Victory** | Fame, gold and experience. Bosses give three times as much. |
| 💀 **Defeat** | You lose 10% of your gold and 5 fame, but gain 5 XP. |

Winning by a hair or without a scratch brings you closer to some [titles](#titles).

## Skills

By level 30 you learn **24 skills**, but you can only **equip 6 at once**. You decide which ones to take under **📜 Manage skills** on the castle screen.

- The **Sword Strike** is your basic attack. It is always available, doesn't count towards the 6 slots and can be used as often as you like.
- When you learn a new skill and have a free slot, it is equipped automatically. Otherwise you can swap it in yourself.
- In battle only your equipped skills are available. **Ether potions** also recharge only these.

> 💡 Tailor your selection to the enemy: against bosses, Intimidate, Pierce and Execution pay off; in the tower, Vampire Strike and Second Wind; against poison and fire foes, Purification.

|---|---|---|

## Foes

On adventures, in tournaments and in the tower you meet different kinds of foes:

| Foe | Special trait |
|---|---|
| **Knight** | Balanced, sometimes takes cover |
| **Bandit** | Little armour, poisons you with the poison dagger ☠️ |
| **Fire Knight** | Sets you ablaze with the burning blow 🔥 |
| **Armoured Knight** | Lots of HP and thick armour, stuns with the hammer 💫 |
| **Archer** | Little HP, but shoots hard, especially with the arrow volley |
| **Mage** | Fragile, but dangerous with fireballs (burn) and frost bolts (stun) |

Foes can be **men or women**, so you will also meet dames and female bandits and archers. Their gear depends on their level. Bandits and archers wear lighter armour, armoured knights heavy armour.

**Enchanted foes:** from level 6 some foes carry enchanted weapons and armour, from level 20 all of them. You can tell by their glowing weapons and armour, and it's mentioned in the text at the start of the fight. The items work for them too:
- Weapons can **set you on fire, poison or paralyse** you, land **critical** hits more often or **steal HP** for them.
- Armour gives them **more HP**, lets them **regenerate** or **dodge**. Some make them **immune to fire**.

There are twelve bosses that you must defeat one after another. Each boss requires a certain amount of **fame** before you can challenge it. A boss fight costs 3 energy, and you cannot flee.

| # | Boss | Fame required | Dangerous because of |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bandit Captain Greyhead | 40 | Poison |
| 2 | The Black Knight | 150 | Stun, hard blows |
| 3 | Dragon Lord Vulkanus | 500 | Fire breath and Inferno |
| 4 | Ice Queen Frostara | 1000 | Frost stun, Blizzard |
| 5 | Lich Mortrax | 2000 | Poison cloud, Death ray |
| 6 | Demon Prince Azgaroth | 4000 | Hellfire, Doom |
| 7 | Storm Giant Thunderfist | 6000 | Thunderclap stun, Chain lightning |
| 8 | Witch Queen Morgana | 8500 | Curse mist (poison), Soulfire (burn) |
| 9 | Kraken Lord Abyssor | 12000 | Stranglehold (stun), Ink venom, Tidal wave |
| 10 | Phoenix Emperor Ignarion | 16000 | Firestorm, Ash rain |
| 11 | Shadow Empress Nyx | 21000 | Nightmare (stun), the devastating Void |
| 12 | The World Eater | 28000 | Everything at once: fire, poison, stun and World's End |

> 💡 From boss 7 on, it pays to use the crafts. Upgraded gear, runes and home-brewed potions make the difference.

> ⚠️ **Watch out:** when a boss drops below half its HP, it **flies into a rage** and hits much harder.

> 🗡️ **Mercenary:** if you have enough fame for the next boss, you can **hire a mercenary** on the castle screen. He fights at your side in the next boss fight and strikes every round.

> 👑 **The King's War Chest:** from the sixth defeated boss on, you can give gold to the King's war chest **once a day** on the castle screen. > 📜 Every boss has its own story. It is told before the fight, and after your victory you learn how it ends. After the World Eater an **epilogue** awaits.

**Reward for each boss:**
- triple combat reward
- **two pieces of [random loot](#enchanted-gear)**, at least 🔵 Rare
- a greater healing potion ⚗️ and an antidote 💊
- from boss 3: the Endless Tower is unlocked.

## The inventory

In the **🎒 Inventory** you see everything your knight owns:

- **Equipped:** weapon, armour and shield with their values, upgrades, runes (✦) and special powers
- **Combat stats:** damage, protection, max. HP, crit chance, dodge and all extra effects such as burn, poison or lifesteal. You see at once what a change of gear brings.
- **🎒 Bag:** your random loot. Here you equip or unequip it, compare it with your gear and salvage what you don't need (see [Enchanted gear](#enchanted-gear)).
- **Special weapons and armour:** forged items and former fixed loot pieces. You equip and unequip them here too. If you unequip them, you fight with your bought gear again.
- **Potions:** your supply. Potions are used in battle.
- **Materials:** everything you have gathered for the crafts
- **Trophies:** trophies, defeated bosses, titles and tower record

## Enchanted gear

You can't buy enchanted weapons, armour and shields – you win them as loot. Every piece of loot is **rolled at random**: what type, how strong and which special powers. Two finds with the same name can therefore be very different in quality. You can also see them on your knight, by the glowing weapon and armour.

### Rarity

| Rarity | Special powers |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Uncommon | 1 |
| 🔵 Rare | 2 |
| 🟣 Epic | 3 |
| 🟠 Legendary | 4 |

Higher rarity also brings a slightly higher base value.

### What is rolled

- **Type:** about half are weapons, from all seven [weapon types](#the-market), plus armour and shields. A weapon keeps the effect of its type – an axe, for example, always causes bleeding.
- **Base value:** damage for weapons, protection for armour and shields. It depends on the **level of the find**: the tougher the enemy, the stronger the loot.
- **Special powers:**
  - Weapons: burn, poison, paralysis, crit chance, lifesteal
  - Armour and shields: max. HP, healing per round, dodge, fire immunity

  Shields have somewhat weaker powers than armour.

*Lifesteal*: part of your damage heals you.

### Where loot comes from

| Source | Loot | Rarity |
|---|---|---|
| 🏟️ Tournament win | 1 piece, 2 on tournament day | mostly 🟢 or 🔵, rarely 🟣 or 🟠 |
| 🗼 Tower boss (every 5th floor) | 1 piece | gets better with every floor, from floor 30 more often 🟠 |
| 🗼 Every 10th floor | 1 piece | like a tower boss, but one rarity better |
| 👑 Boss | 2 pieces | at least 🔵, good chance of 🟣 |
| 🌕 Full-moon werewolf | 1 piece | at least 🔵 |

### Equipping and comparing

- With every find you see at once what it changes compared with your equipped gear, for example **⚔️ ▲ +6 · Crit ▼ −5%**. The comparison includes the upgrades of your equipped item.
- If you're not wearing anything enchanted in a slot yet and the find is better, **it is equipped automatically**. Otherwise it goes into the **🎒 bag** in your inventory, and you decide yourself.
- Random loot can be **upgraded** and fitted with **runes** like any gear, shields included (+1 protection per upgrade, armour runes).

### The bag and salvaging

The bag holds **20 pieces**. Equipped gear doesn't count. If it overflows, nothing is lost, but **tower, tournament and boss fights are locked** until you tidy up.

What you don't need you **salvage** into materials, one at a time or with **Salvage all 🟢** / **Salvage all 🔵**:

| Rarity | Yield |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Uncommon | 2–3 iron ore or leather |
| 🔵 Rare | 1–2 arcane crystals |
| 🟣 Epic | 2 mithril ore and 2 arcane crystals |
| 🟠 Legendary | 1 dragon scale and 2 mithril ore |

If an item has been upgraded, you get half of the materials spent back. Runes are lost when salvaging. Equipped gear can't be salvaged. Upgraded items and items with runes are skipped by the bulk buttons – you salvage those one at a time.

> 💡 Former fixed loot pieces such as the Flame Blade, the Dragon Fang or the World Blade no longer drop. If you already own them, you keep them along with upgrades and runes.

## Materials and the trader

For the crafts you need **materials**. You get them in two ways:

1. **Winning fights.** After every victory you find 1–2 materials. Which ones depends on the foe.
2. **Buying from the 🛒 trader.** He sells all materials and buys back spares for 40% of the price.

| Material | Price at the trader | Found mainly with |
|---|---|---|
| ⛏️ Iron ore | 8 🪙 | knights, armoured knights, fire knights |
| 🟫 Leather | 8 🪙 | bandits, archers |
| 🌿 Healing herb | 6 🪙 | archers, bandits, knights |
| 🍄 Toadstool | 8 🪙 | bandits, archers |
| 🌺 Fire blossom | 15 🪙 | fire knights |
| 💎 Arcane crystal | 20 🪙 | knights, fire knights, bosses |
| 🪨 Mithril ore | 35 🪙 | armoured knights, strong foes (from level 8), bosses |
| 🐉 Dragon scale | 150 🪙 | bosses, rarely the full-moon werewolf and Sir Gregor, in the tower only on every 10th floor |

Bosses drop especially much: dragon scales, arcane crystals, mithril ore and herbs. Dragon scales remain rare, though. The tower bosses on floors 5, 15, 25 and so on give none.

## Crafts – the workshop

In the **🧰 Workshop** you practise three crafts. You can learn all three at once. Each craft has its own **level from 1 to 10**.

- **Cost:** every craft costs **1 energy** and the materials shown. Some recipes also cost gold.
- **Craft XP:** with every item you make you collect craft XP. When the craft levels up, you unlock new recipes.
- **Cost display:** anything you're missing is shown in red.

### 🔨 Blacksmith

**Upgrade:** you can strengthen every weapon and armour in your inventory by up to **+5**, enchanted and forged ones too. You **don't need to equip** them for this. The workshop lists everything you own and marks what you're currently wearing.
- Each level on the weapon gives **+2 damage**, each level on armour **+1 protection**.
- How far you can upgrade depends on your smith level: +1 from level 1, +2 from level 3, +3 from level 5, +4 from level 7, +5 from level 9.
- Higher upgrades need mithril ore, +5 also a dragon scale.
- Upgrades stay with the item. If you buy a new weapon, it starts at +0 again.
- **Legendary smithing:** from smith level 10 you can upgrade up to **+10**. That costs a lot of gold, mithril ore and dragon scales.

## The tavern

In the **🍺 tavern “The Golden Tankard”** you play **dice poker** for gold. Cheerful pub music plays, and the innkeeper tells you rumours that contain useful tips.

### How it works
1. Choose an **opponent** and your **stake**.
2. You and your opponent each roll **5 dice**.
3. **Tap the dice** you want to reroll (they're marked red) and reroll once. You can also keep them all.
4. Then your opponent rerolls. He sees what you have.
5. The better combination wins.

| Rank | Combination | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Five of a Kind | ⚅⚅⚅⚅⚅ |
| 2 | Four of a Kind | ⚃⚃⚃⚃⚀ |
| 3 | Full House | ⚂⚂⚂⚄⚄ |
| 4 | Large Straight | ⚁⚂⚃⚄⚅ |
| 5 | Small Straight | ⚀⚁⚂⚃⚄ |
| 6 | Three of a Kind | ⚄⚄⚄⚀⚁ |
| 7 | Two Pairs | ⚁⚁⚃⚃⚅ |
| 8 | One Pair | ⚅⚅⚀⚂⚃ |
| 9 | Nothing | – |

With the same combination, the higher pips decide, e.g. a pair of sixes against a pair of twos.

**Winnings:** a win returns your stake plus the same amount. If you win with **four of a kind**, the opponent pays **one and a half times**, with **five of a kind** **three times**. On a complete tie you get your stake back.

### The opponents
| Opponent | Stakes | Playing style | Available |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🍺 Farmer Gustl | 10 / 25 | rerolls aimlessly, easy to beat | right away |
| 🎲 Gambler Rosalind | 25 / 50 / 100 | solid and thoughtful | from level 3 |
| 🗡️ Mercenary Brakk | 50 / 100 / 250 | greedy, always chases the biggest combination | from level 6 |
| 🎩 Count of Dicestone | 100 / 250 / 500 | calculates exactly what he needs to beat you | from rank Baron |

You can play **at most 5 games per day**.

🍻 **A round for everyone:** once a day you can buy the whole tavern a round. It costs gold but brings fame, and the innkeeper tells you two rumours at once. Against Gustl, dicing almost always pays. Against the others it roughly breaks even with good rerolling – more thrill than gold mine.

> 💡 Keep pairs and triples, reroll the rest. With four dice of a straight (e.g. 2-3-4-5) it's worth rerolling only the fifth.

## The Endless Tower

From the third defeated boss on, the Endless Tower is open to you. A visit costs 4 energy.

- You fight your way up **floor by floor**, and the foes keep getting stronger.
- After every floor you recover **20% of your HP**. Limited skills don't refill on their own. For that you need **🔷 ether potions**.
- With a little luck (25%) you find an **ether potion** after a floor.
- **Every 5th floor** is guarded by a boss.
- **Every tower boss** drops a piece of [random loot](#enchanted-gear) – the higher the floor, the better.
- **Every 10th floor** brings loot one rarity better, plus a **greater ether potion**.
- You can **leave** the tower at any time. Next visit you continue on the floor where you stopped. You then start with full HP and fully recharged skills, but a visit costs 4 energy again.
- **If you are defeated**, you start again on **floor 1** next time.
- Your highest floor is saved as your **tower record**. If a tower run ends in defeat, it goes into the **🗼 Hall of Fame** in the inventory, which shows your five best runs.

### The levels of the tower

After every tower boss you enter a new, ever more menacing level:

| Floors | Level | What awaits you |

| 6–10 | ⛓️ Dungeons | Chains, mould and dripping water |
| 11–15 | 💎 Crystal Cave | Icy glowing crystals |
| 16–20 | 🌋 Lava Halls | Bubbling lava and flying sparks |
| 21–25 | 👻 Ghost Halls | Green ghost flames and floating spectres |

Every level has its **own music and soundscape**: crackling torches, dripping water and rattling chains, singing crystals, rumbling lava, whispering ghosts, rain and thunder, a menacing drone in the Shadow Realm and cosmic sounds in the World Rift. When you enter a new level, a deep gong sounds.

## Rebirth

Once you have defeated all twelve bosses, you can be **reborn**.

**You lose:** level, gold (you start with 50), fame, bought gear (including its upgrades and runes), and your progress against the bosses and the rival.

**You keep:** enchanted and forged items including your bag (with upgrades and runes), potions, materials, craft levels, castle and decorations, companions, trophies, tower record, titles and everything unlocked.

**You permanently gain:** +10% damage, +10% gold and +10% experience. All foes become 8% stronger, though.

**On top, you choose a blessing each time:**

## Settings and saves

- **✏️ Rename**: click the pencil next to your name and enter a new name (up to 20 characters).
- **♂️ / ♀️ Knight or dame**: a click on the symbol next to your name switches between knight and dame. Look and rank adapt.
- **🌐 Language**: at the very bottom of the castle screen, next to the save slots, you switch between Deutsch and English. On first start the language follows your browser. The setting applies to all slots, and your save is kept.
- **🎵 Music / 🔊 Sound**: the buttons at the bottom right turn music and sound effects on or off. The music adapts to the situation: calm at the castle, hammer blows in the workshop, fanfares at the tournament, drums in battle and a dark choir for bosses. When a boss flies into a rage, the music speeds up. When your HP runs low you hear your heartbeat.
- **🗣️ Voice**: the third button at the bottom right turns **speech output** on or off. When it's on, new messages are read aloud: messages at the castle, the course of battle, the innkeeper's rumours, dice results and the night report. Which voice speaks depends on your device and browser. Speech output only starts after you have clicked somewhere once.
- **Light / dark**: the game follows your device's setting. In dark mode it plays at night under stars and moon.
- **Saving**: your progress is saved automatically in your browser. On another device or in another browser you start from scratch. If you clear your browser data, the save is gone.
- **💾 Save slots**: at the very bottom of the castle screen you manage up to **3 saves**, for example for different knights or dames. For each slot you see name, rank, level, day, gold and when you last played. You can **load** a slot, **start new** on an empty slot or **delete** a slot. Deleting must be confirmed a second time and can't be undone. The game always saves automatically to the active slot.
- **📤 Back up & transfer**: on the save screen you can **save the active save as a file** or **copy it as text** and **import** it again later, for example on another device. That way your knight isn't lost even if you clear your browser data.
- **📱 Install as an app**: via the browser menu (e.g. “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app”) the game becomes an app with its own icon. Once loaded, it can also be played **offline**.
- **ℹ️ Version**: the version number is shown at the bottom of the save screen. The game is a **prototype**: bugs and balance changes are possible, and saves may break with new versions. So back up your knight regularly with “Save as file”.
- **💡 Tips**: the first time you open a new feature, a short tip card explains the essentials.
- **Expand and collapse stats**: tap the summary line with level, energy, gold and fame to see all stats. Music, sound, voice and language settings apply to all slots.

> 💡 If you leave a fight in progress, for example by reloading the page, the fight is lost. The energy spent and potions drunk are gone.

## Tips for beginners

1. **On the first day** it's best to go on 2 adventures and work twice. An iron sword for 40 gold pays off early.
2. **Buy gear in the order weapon, armour, shield.** A faster victory means less damage taken.
3. **Always carry 2–3 healing potions.** Especially for tournaments and bosses.
4. **Take antidotes to Greyhead, Vulkanus, Mortrax, Azgaroth, Morgana, Abyssor, Ignarion and the World Eater.** Poison and burns add up quickly.
5. **War Cry and the Strength Elixir last 3 rounds.** Use your strongest skills right after.
6. **Against bosses, Iron Stance pays off** just before they fly into a rage.
7. **Tournaments bring your first random loot early.** A find with dodge or healing per round is very valuable at the start.
8. **Don't sell dragon scales.** You need them for the best recipes, and at the trader they cost 150 gold.
9. **Alchemy pays off early.** Healing herbs are cheap, and healing potions are the most important ingredient for tough fights.
10. **In the tower** you should get out in time when your HP runs low. Your record is kept.
11. **Take ether potions into the tower.** Without them you'll only have the Sword Strike left after a few floors.

Good luck, noble knight! 🛡️⚔️
