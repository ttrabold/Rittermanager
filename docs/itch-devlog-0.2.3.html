Version 0.2.3 is out, and it adds something my son has been asking for since the first version: weapons that feel special.

The idea

Until now loot was rolled: a sword with some fire, a bow with some poison. Fine, but nothing you would remember. Now there are 21 unique weapons, three for each weapon type (sword, axe, club, halberd, bow, staff, dagger). Each one has its own look and an effect you won't find on any normal item.

Effects that change how you fight

  • Double Strike: sometimes you hit twice.
  • Time Rift: sometimes the enemy loses its turn.
  • Chain Lightning: critical hits jump over as a lightning bolt.
  • Laser Beam: an energy beam shoots at the enemy (this one was my son's idea).
  • Phoenix Heart: once per fight you get back up at 0 HP.
  • Return, Soul Drain, Bloodlust, Mirror Blade, Mercy Strike, Armour Breaker, Storm Call and Transformation round it off.

Every effect has its own animation in the fight, so you really see it when it happens.

Finding one

Unique weapons drop rarely from tower bosses and bosses, and even more rarely from tournaments, the mimic and the werewolf. When you find one, there is a short reveal: lightning hits a chest, the lid flies open and the weapon spins out. You can find the same weapon more than once, and it gets stronger with your level, so a later find is better than an early one.

My favourites: Thunderfang, a five-pronged sword with lightning dancing between its tips, and the Sun Laser, a staff with a little sun in it that charges up before it fires.

Balance

I tuned it with a simulation: on average a unique weapon wins a bit more fights than a legendary of the same type and level, but some are weaker than others. Please tell me which one feels too strong or too weak.

Your save

Your save carries over. If you want to go back to an older version, keep in mind that older versions don't know these weapons and show them as ordinary items. Backing up your save (Save slots, "Save as file") is still a good idea while this is a prototype.
