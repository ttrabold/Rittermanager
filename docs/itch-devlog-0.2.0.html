Version 0.2.0 is out, and it's all about the Endless Tower.

Why the tower

My son had climbed the tower a lot and said what I had been ignoring: it gets boring. Every fifth floor you met a boss, but they were just the twelve bosses you had already beaten in the main game, one after another. And from floor 36 on, every floor looked and sounded the same. So that's what this update fixes.

20 new tower bosses

The tower now has its own bosses that you won't meet anywhere else, one every five floors up to floor 100. Each one has its own look and at least one trick you have to deal with:

  • Wind-up: the boss draws back for a huge blow. Take cover or stun it.
  • Minions: your hits land on the minions first. Hit several times, or use poison and fire, which still reach the boss.
  • Mirror shield: for two turns half your damage bounces back. Heal or buff instead of hitting.
  • Transformation: at half health the boss changes shape and fights harder.
  • Rebirth: once per fight it gets back up.
  • Seal: a curse locks one of your skills for a while.

The tricks are always announced in the fight log, so you can react. Higher up, bosses combine two or three of them. My favourite is Your Shadow Twin on floor 35, who looks exactly like your own knight, only darker. The last one, on floor 100, is the Tower Lord.

12 new levels

Every five floors you still reach a new level, but now it keeps going all the way to 100: Clockwork Chambers, a Sunken Library, a Mushroom Grotto, a Frozen Crypt, a Blood Rose Garden, a Bone Crypt, a Sky Forge, a Hall of Mirrors, the Dreamlands, an Observatory, a Golden Hoard and finally the Tower Crown. Each one has its own sounds.

After floor 100

The tower starts over as a shadow run: darker levels and stronger shadow versions of the bosses. Every further hundred floors makes them tougher.

Balance

Up to floor 60 the new bosses are roughly as hard as the old ones on the same floor. Above that it gets harder step by step. I tuned it with a simulation, so real fights may feel different. Please tell me if a boss is unfair or too easy.

Your save

Your save and your current tower floor carry over. If you were in the middle of a climb, you continue on the same floor, just with the new bosses and levels. Backing up your save (Save slots → Save as file) is still a good idea while this is a prototype.

Thanks for playing, and let me know which boss beat you!
