**Title:** Sir Grindalot 0.1.0 is playable

**Tags:** Release, Prototype, RPG

---

```
The first version of Sir Grindalot is up. It's 0.1.0, which means it's an early prototype and some things are still rough. But it's playable from start to finish, and I wanted to get it out there instead of polishing forever.

You can play it in the browser, on a PC or on your phone. It's in English and German.

What it is

You start as a squire with a wooden sword and no armour. Each day you get a bit of energy and decide what to do with it: work for gold, go on adventures, fight in tournaments, craft, build up your castle. Over time you level up, get better gear and take on twelve bosses. The name says it: there's some grinding involved, on purpose.

Made with my son

I'm making this game together with my 9-year-old son. He plays every version, tells me when something is boring or too hard, and a lot of the ideas are his. Many features only exist because he asked "Papa, can we add...?" and I said yes. He'll read the comments too, so be nice.

What's in 0.1.0

- 12 bosses, each with a short backstory
- Turn-based fights with 24 skills, potions, poison, burns and stuns
- Random loot in four rarities, with a quick comparison to what you're wearing
- Blacksmith, alchemy and runes
- A castle that grows and gives you resources every night
- Dice poker in the tavern, a rival knight called Sir Gregor, companions, a werewolf at full moon
- An endless tower, and a rebirth option once you've beaten everything
- You can play as a knight or a dame

Please back up your save

Since this is a prototype, saves can break when I put out a new version. You can export your save in the game under Save slots, "Save as file". The version number is shown there as well.

Feedback

This is the best time to tell me what you think. Was something too easy or too hard? Did you find a bug? What did you like, and what was missing? Just leave a comment.

What's next

Mostly balancing and fixing things. The loot needs some tuning, and the fight screen has a layout issue on small phones. Later I'd like to add achievements, a stats screen and maybe a weekly tower challenge with a leaderboard.

Thanks for trying it out!
```
