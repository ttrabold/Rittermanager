Hear ye, hear ye! ⚔️

Sir Grindalot – From Squire to Legend is now playable for the very first time. Version 0.1.0 is an early, work-in-progress prototype – rough around the edges, but already packed with things to do. You can play it right here in your browser, on desktop or on your phone, in English or German.

## What is Sir Grindalot?

You start as a humble squire with a wooden sword and nothing but rags. Every day gives you a handful of energy, and how you spend it is up to you: work for coin, ride out on adventures, enter tournaments, brew potions, build up your castle – and slowly, stubbornly, grind your way up from squire to Immortal Legend.

## A father-and-son project

Sir Grindalot is developed in close collaboration with my 9-year-old son. He's the lead play-tester, the toughest balance critic and the source of many of the game's ideas – from which bosses the knight has to face to what a proper castle needs. A lot of features exist simply because he asked "Papa, can we add…?" and the answer was yes.

So if you enjoy the game, half the credit goes to him. And if you leave a comment, he'll be reading it too. 😊

## What's in this first version

• 12 bosses, each with their own story – from a bandit captain all the way to the World Eater
• Turn-based combat with 24 skills, potions, burns, poison, stuns and bosses that fly into a rage when they're losing
• Randomly rolled loot in four rarities (Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary) with an instant comparison against your current gear
• Three crafts: forge and upgrade gear up to legendary +10, brew potions, inscribe runes
• A castle that grows from a homestead into a royal castle and pays out every night – if you've worked hard enough that day
• Dice poker in the tavern, a jealous rival named Sir Gregor, companions, a werewolf hunt at full moon
• The Endless Tower for when you think you've seen it all, and rebirth to start over even stronger
• Play as a knight or a dame, optional voice narration, music that changes with the situation

## ⚠️ Please read: this is a prototype

Things will break. Numbers will change. And with new versions, your save might not survive the update.

Please back up your knight regularly: in the game, open 💾 Save slots and use "Save as file". The current version number is shown there too, so you always know what you're playing.

## I'd love your feedback

This is exactly the stage where your feedback shapes the game the most. Tell me in the comments:

• Was anything too easy, too hard or too grindy (beyond the intended amount of grind 😉)?
• Did you find a bug? What were you doing when it happened?
• Which feature did you enjoy most – and what did you miss?

## What's next

The next updates will focus on polish and balance: fine-tuning the random loot, making sure the late game stays challenging, and smoothing out layout issues on small screens. Further down the road I'm thinking about achievements, a statistics screen and a weekly tower challenge with a leaderboard.

Thanks for stopping by, and good luck out there, noble knight. The kingdom isn't going to save itself. 🛡️
